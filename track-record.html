The formula

The same math, every time, for every material

No AI black box, no proprietary model. One formula, applied consistently:

Landed Cost = Material Price × (1 + Tariff %) + Freight

That's it. The same formula runs on the free calculator, the dashboard, and the paid alerts. Nothing hidden behind "our proprietary algorithm."

Where the data comes from

Real sources, named directly

METALS

Aluminum & Copper

Live spot prices from a market data API tracking LME/COMEX benchmarks.

STEEL & RESIN

Index-Scaled Estimates

No free live spot-price feed exists for these, so we scale a real government price index (FRED Producer Price Index) against a spot price we've verified — clearly labeled as an estimate, not a live quote.

LUMBER

Index-Scaled Estimate

Same method as steel — scaled from FRED's lumber PPI series, calibrated against a real checked spot price.

FREIGHT

Real Formula, Real Fuel Data

Uses the industry-standard fuel surcharge formula with the real national average diesel price from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) — a generic estimate, not your specific carrier's rate.

TARIFFS

Federal Register

New steel/aluminum trade policy notices are pulled directly from the U.S. Federal Register — a free, public, no-key-required government source.

CURRENCY

FRED FX Rates

Daily exchange rate data from the Federal Reserve's public FRED database — genuinely free, no subscription.
